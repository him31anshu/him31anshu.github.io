About Me

My research follows an interdisciplinary trajectory spanning metallurgy and materials science, aerospace engineering, elastic-wave propagation and non-destructive evaluation, and structural health monitoring. I began with an experimental foundation in metallurgical and materials engineering, before moving into aerospace engineering during my PhD, where I developed a research focus on understanding how material microstructure influences elastic-wave behaviour.


My doctoral research focused on microstructure-informed elastic wave propagation in textured polycrystalline materials. I investigated how crystallographic texture, grain structure and material heterogeneity influence wave propagation, and developed computationally efficient approaches combining synthetic microstructures, experimental EBSD data, finite-element modelling and wave-based inverse analysis.


I am working with Prof. S. Gopalakrishnan on an Aeronautics Research & Development Board (ARDB)-sponsored project focused on in-situ stress-corrosion monitoring. The specific contribution involves the design and architectural development of an experimental test rig integrating tensile, fatigue, and torsional loading with a multimodal sensing scheme based on hyperspectral imaging (HSI), digital image correlation (DIC), X-ray diffraction (XRD), and extensometry. The platform is being developed to enable real-time characterisation of material degradation and stress-corrosion state

Research Highlight

Elastic Wave Propagation Through Heterogeneous Microstructures

A numerical comparison of elastic wave propagation through a homogeneous medium and a heterogeneous polycrystalline volume element (PVE), illustrating the influence of grain boundaries and microstructural heterogeneity on wave propagation.

The heterogeneous microstructure introduces scattering at grain boundaries, producing characteristic variations in the propagating wave field compared with the homogeneous case.

Elastic wave propagation through homogeneous and heterogeneous polycrystalline media, illustrating grain-boundary scattering.
